annotation: >-
  Low-emission, limited-traffic, pedestrian and parking zones are spreading across European cities,
  yet many authorities cannot publish them as data under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/670. UVAR Box
  lets them draw a zone, define rules from templates and export validated DATEX II without specialist
  knowledge. Proven in an EU pilot, it is being modernised in NAPCORE during 2027.
