  Spatiall by AI-InfraSolutions captures 360° imagery and LiDAR of the Dutch road network every year.
  AI detects traffic signs, road markings and other features, and compares them with the authority’s
  asset register. The road authority accepts or rejects each change, and only validated data goes to
  the NAP (NDW). Less field work, register and NAP aligned.
