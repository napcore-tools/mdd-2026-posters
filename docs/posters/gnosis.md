annotation: >-
  GNOSIS is an advanced GIS-based digital platform for modeling, monitoring, analyzing, and
  visualizing Cyprus’s transport infrastructure. Integrating road networks, sensors, and connected
  systems, it supports intelligent transport planning and asset management. Through digital twin, 3D
  visualization, and easy data exchange via CyNAP, GNOSIS enhances stakeholder coordination,
  infrastructure resilience, sustainability, and compliance with European Intelligent Transport
  Systems (ITS) requirements.
