be made accessible via National Access Points.

The Context Low emission (LEZ), limited traffic (LTZ), pedestrian (PED) and parking zones (PARK)
keep multiplying across European cities. Drivers, fleets and navigation apps need these rules as
data before and during the trip. Your duty as an authority Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/670 (Art.
5) brings access regulations into the data to be shared via National Access Points, in DATEX II or
TN-ITS. Many authorities lack the resources or DATEX II skills to do this alone.

How it works 4 steps for an authority

The solution: UVAR Box 1. Outline the Zone: draw the zone or import a Shapefile. 2. Define the
Rules: map-based web tool with ready-made templates per zone type: LEZ, LTZ, PED, PARK. 3. Validate
as DATEX II: each UVAR is checked against the DATEX II UVAR profile on import and on save. 4. Export
Publish: export standard DATEX II, ready to be published via the NAP and reused by navigation
services. No DATEX II expertise needed the authority stays responsible for its rules.

Original UVAR Box interface (EU pilot, 2022): map of zones and list of digitised UVARs.
