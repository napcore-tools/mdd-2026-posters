How it works - 4 steps for an authority

The solution: UVAR Box
1. Outline the Zone: draw the zone or import a Shapefile.
2. Define the Rules: map-based web tool with ready-made
templates per zone type: LEZ, LTZ, PED, PARK.

3. Validate as DATEX II: each UVAR is checked against the
DATEX II UVAR profile on import and on save.
4. Export & Publish: export standard DATEX II, ready to be
published via the NAP and reused by navigation services.
No DATEX II expertise needed - the authority stays
responsible for its rules.
