FlowSuite
Traffic Data & Events Intelligent Processing, end-to-end

Is your traffic data good

FlowSuite gives road authorities, cities and traffic operators one platform to monitor, analyse, manage and guide traffic
across their entire network. Every step relies on reliable traffic event data. The Quality Dashboard in FlowImpact shows
how your feed scores on the TISA RTTI 5-star scale and what to improve to reach the level service providers commit to.
